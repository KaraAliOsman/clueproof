# Stranger's File

A single photo rarely says much. A stranger who sees many of a family's shared photos can put the clues together. The Stranger's File makes that visible: eight pages a stranger could try to fill, and which shared photos fill them.

## The eight pages

| Page | What a stranger learns | Clues that fill it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 Where you live | The street or the door of your home | ADR, MAL |
| 2 Your children | Where your children study or what they are called | SCH, RUL for a named child |
| 3 Your car | Which car is yours | PLT |
| 4 Where you go | Where photos were taken, and trips you booked | LOC, TKT |
| 5 Who you are | Identity documents, payment cards, your name stored in the files | IDD, PAY, MET with an owner, artist or copyright name (a camera serial alone does not fill it) |
| 6 How to get in | Keys, passwords, codes and open accounts | KEY, COD, SCR |
| 7 Your health | Medicines and medical details | MED |
| 8 Other people | People who appear without having chosen to | FAC, RUL for other named people |

A page is **filled** when at least one confirmed finding belongs to it, and **worth a look** when only possible findings do. The headline counts filled pages only: "A stranger could fill 5 of 8 pages about your family."

## Worst together

Some pages reveal much more together. Report a combination only when every page it needs is filled, and say which photos create it.

| Pages filled | What they reveal together |
| --- | --- |
| 1 and 2 | Where your children live and where they study |
| 1 and 6 | Where you live and how to get in |
| 1 and 4 | Where you live and when you are away from home (only when a TKT finding fills page 4) |
| 1 and 5 | Enough to pose as you or send mail in your name |
| 1 and 3 | Which car belongs to which house |

Show at most the two worst combinations in the answer, in the order of this table; give the rest on request.

## Rules

- Use the Public lens: the question is what anyone could learn if the shared albums reached them.
- Name pages, clue types, photo files and albums. Never state the value behind a clue (no street, school, plate, code, name or place).
- List a photo under every page it fills.
- Photos in a Keep private album are read and counted like any other while they are still in a shared album, because the mark does not remove them from it.
- Say how many shared photos were read. When some were not read, never call a page empty: say "nothing found in the 20 photos read" and how many photos are left for the next run.
- The GPS count covers every listed photo, because metadata is read for up to 100 photos.
- A page closes only when its photos stop being shared; a new Stranger's File shows the change.
