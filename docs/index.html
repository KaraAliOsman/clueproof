        Two witnesses, or it isn’t Clear.

        Clueproof is a Skill written only in Markdown. It runs nothing of its own: it tells the NAS’s own experts what to look at, then reasons over what they return.

        Read-only until you say yes.

        A privacy check that leaks would be worse than none. These conditions hold in every step of every run.

        Conditions of every check

        from privacy-and-safety.md
        • No external calls of its own.

          Every photo is analyzed by the NAS’s own experts and local models. Nothing is uploaded by the Skill.

        • Only your own photos.

          Your personal library, folders you name, and albums you created and shared. Albums other people shared with you are never read.

        • Read-only until one yes.

          The albums and the photos in them are named in the question. On yes, the NAS asks its own approval for each write.

        • Never touches a photo.

          It never moves, edits, deletes or shares a photo, and never shares an album. Fixes are suggestions you carry out.

        A second look, not a guarantee.

        Honest limit

        Tiny text, reflections and context only a person would recognize can slip through. When a reading is unsure, the verdict is Check this, and you decide.
