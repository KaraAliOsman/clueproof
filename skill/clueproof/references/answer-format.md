# Answer format

Clueproof answers like a friend who just looked over your shoulder: the result first, in three short lines, then one yes-or-no question that fixes everything. Details only on request.

## First line (Step 3)

"Checking 12 photos from today as if they were posted publicly (say family only to change that). They are read only by your NAS's own models. GPS location is stored in 1 of them. Full answer in about 6 minutes."

Replace the minutes with the time measured on the testing day. For the Stranger's File: "Summer 2025 hands out the GPS location of 1 of its 12 photos to everyone you shared it with. Reading the photos now."

During the readings, one progress line per batch: "10 of 12 read, 3 clues so far."

## Quick check answer (Step 8)

Example with the test album "Summer 2025", audience Public:

```text
6 of 12 photos are ready to post (S01 still carries GPS location: post it through an app that removes location).
4 give clues away: house number (S05), boarding pass (S07), school crest (S08), address label (S10). 2 need a fix: license plate (S06), keys (S11).
Nothing was skipped.
Put the 6 ready photos in a ready-to-share album and keep the 4 risky ones in a private Keep private album?
```

When the user asks "why?" or "details", one line per photo:

```text
Keep private · S05-house-number.jpg · House number visible, top right (text and vision) · Crop it out or leave this photo out.
Fix first · S06-license-plate.jpg · License plate readable, bottom center (text and vision) · Crop or blur the plate.
```

Rules:

- Name the clue type, never its value: "house number", not the number; "GPS location stored", never a place name.
- In the three lines, name each photo by its short name (the part before the first dash); when the name is only a camera number, add the time taken, such as IMG_4821 (14:32).
- Photos with verdict Check this go on the second line as "1 to look at yourself: possible boarding pass (S09)"; they are neither added to an album nor marked.
- Say "need a fix", never "need a small fix".
- Say "Nothing was skipped" only when every photo was fully read; otherwise "2 photos could not be fully checked; look at them yourself" and list them.
- Zero clues: "All 12 photos are ready to post. Put them in a ready-to-share album?"

## Stranger's File answer (Step F3)

Example with the shared album "Summer 2025":

```text
A stranger could fill 5 of 8 pages about your family from Summer 2025 (all 12 photos read).
Worst together: where your children live and study (S05, S08), and where you live and how to get in (S05, S11).
Filled: Where you live (S05, S10) · Your children (S08) · Your car (S06) · Where you go (S01, S07) · How to get in (S11).
Make a clean copy of Summer 2025 with the 5 photos that fill no page, to share instead, and show how to stop sharing the old one?
```

Rules:

- Line 1: pages filled, album, photos read. Line 2: at most the two worst combinations. Line 3: filled pages with their photos. Then the one question.
- Use album names only when there is more than one album, and list "worth a look" pages only on request.
- Never state the street, school, plate, code, place or person behind a clue.

## Album names

- Ready to share: `Ready to share - <label> - <YYYY-MM-DD>`, for example `Ready to share - Summer 2025 - 2026-11-20`; the label is Today or Newest 20 for a default selection.
- Clean copy: `Clean copy - <album> - <YYYY-MM-DD>`.
- Memory: `Keep private - <YYYY-MM-DD>`.
- Labels are shortened to 40 characters and never contain street names, house numbers or full names of people. A repeated name gets " - 2".

## After the fix

One line per write and nothing else: "Album Ready to share - Summer 2025 - 2026-11-20 created with 6 photos. 4 photos kept in Keep private - 2026-11-20." Mention any write that failed and what is still pending.
