# Clueproof launch film: script

Length about 2:05, 1920 x 1080, 30 fps. Product-reveal style: a cold open with a mystery, the turn, the reveal, three proof moments, and a closing line. Visual language: the Stranger's File, an investigator's case file (paper, red string, evidence markers, redaction bars), cutting to a clean, modern product interface. Real photographs only (the Summer 2025 and Hazard sets in `docs/assets/photos/`). Music: a real, human-composed, licensed track (credited in `docs/launch-film/CREDITS.md`). Narration: recorded by the team with ElevenLabs from the lines below (see `elevenlabs.md`).

The NAS demo footage required by the Round 2 rules goes into the two marked slots; until then the film uses the interface mock-ups.

## On-screen text rules

- Clue labels name the type only ("house number", "school crest", "boarding pass", "GPS location"), never the value.
- Full English subtitles of the narration, burned in at the bottom third in the final cut.
- No third-party logos; the UGREEN name is used only as text.
